# Asset provenance

The character design is derived from a user-supplied photograph of Bubbles.
The private source photograph is intentionally not committed to this public
repository; only the approved generated derivatives are included.

The photo was translated into a compact, high-detail 3D toy style while
preserving Bubbles' recognizable white coat, rounded face, floppy ears, dark
eyes, black nose, pink collar and heart tag, red waistcoat, white collar, and
black lapels. Dedicated state strips were generated for each animation family.
Running-left was authored as its own row rather than mirrored so the costume
and facial cues remain natural.

All generated strips were normalized to the v2 pet cell geometry, assembled on
a chroma background, decontaminated once at final assembly, and validated for
dimensions, transparency, required occupancy, unused-cell transparency, chroma
leakage, jump lift, look registration, and 16-direction semantics.

Three independent blind reviewers correctly classified every paired
horizontal and vertical direction in the final look loop. The exact reports
used for release are committed under `docs/`.

The original photograph remains the user's content. Generated derivatives are
included for this pet package with the user's authorization in the originating
ChatGPT work session.
